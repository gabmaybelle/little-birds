# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The budgie: a small pet bird in four colours (green, blue, yellow, white), tamed with seeds,
  that flies, sits when told and follows its owner. A spawn egg.
- The budgie's own model and animation: it flaps when it flies, steps and bobs its head on the
  ground, and settles down when told to sit. Textures with the real markings: the wild green's
  yellow face, barred back and violet cheek spots; the blue with a white face; the lutino yellow
  and the albino white with red eyes.
- Wild budgies in savannas and on jungle edges, in small flocks. A settings file
  (`config/little_birds.properties`) turns natural spawning off or makes it rarer.
- The perch, crafted from string, a stick and a plank. A tamed budgie makes the nearest perch its
  home: it keeps near it, sits on it now and then, sleeps on it every night with its head tucked
  in, and stays home when you go out instead of following. Break the perch and it follows you
  again.
- The greeting: when you come back after an hour or more away, your budgie chirps hello and
  bobs. On its perch after dark it goes quiet.
- The budgie's own voice: chatter, a hello chirp and a settling-down warble, several variants of
  each, cut from public-domain recordings of real budgies.
- Shoulder on request: sneak-use a tamed budgie with an empty hand and it sits on your shoulder;
  it never jumps off for a jump or a fall, and it comes with you through portals. Sneak-use it
  again, or sneak-use a block, to set it down. Use a perch to set a shoulder bird down on it, or to call your nearest budgie to it.
