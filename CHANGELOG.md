# Changelog

## Unreleased

- A perch seats its own bird. Using a perch that a bird already calls home calls that bird back
  to it (or does nothing if it's sitting there), rather than handing the perch to your nearest
  bird as well; a bird set down from your shoulder onto another bird's perch hops down at your
  feet instead. Pairs (lovebirds, finches) still share.
- The zebra finch: the smallest little bird, with a red beak, in three colours (the grey with its
  orange cheeks and barred tail, the fawn, the white). Finches like company: one keeps near the
  nearest other finch, two may share one perch and sit on it side by side, and by day a tamed
  finch sings a short phrase now and then; a finch that hears another sing answers it a moment
  later. A millet spray gets a phrase too. It keeps to its perch rather than a shoulder. Its beeps,
  chirps and phrases are cut from recordings of real zebra finches. Wild zebra finches live in
  plains and sunflower plains, always three together (`#little_birds:zebra_finch_spawns`).
- The canary: a song bird a touch smaller than a budgie, in three colours (yellow, the red
  factor, white), with a short straight beak and a notched tail. Every morning a tamed canary
  sings its song, several seconds of it, from wherever it sits; a millet spray gets a song too.
  Its tweets, its hello trill and its song are cut from recordings of real canaries. It keeps to
  its perch: a sneak-use doesn't put it on your shoulder. Tamed with seeds like the rest, with the
  perch, the greeting, the call and chicks all the same. Wild canaries live in flower forests,
  meadows and cherry groves, in twos and threes (`#little_birds:canary_spawns`, for pack makers).
- The millet spray, the one treat, crafted from wheat and seeds: it tames a wild bird on the
  first try, a bird comes to the hand that holds it, and a tamed bird fed one gives a happy bob.
  A treat is not a meal: a bird that has just had one takes nothing more for half a minute.
- Snacks from the farm: a tamed bird nibbles a carrot, an apple, a melon slice, berries or a
  beetroot, crumbs and all, and is the better for it; a chick fed a treat or a snack grows a
  little. Pack makers can change the list (`#little_birds:snacks`).
- No, thank you: cocoa beans and poisonous potatoes get a shake of the head and stay in your
  hand (`#little_birds:refused`).

## 0.2.0: A bird or two

Two more birds, for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric and NeoForge. Worlds from 0.1.0 keep their budgies.

- The cockatiel: a parrot-sized little bird in three colours (the normal grey with its yellow
  face and orange cheeks, the lutino, the white-faced grey), with a crest that stands up when it
  says hello or takes off, and a whistle for a voice, cut from a recording of a real cockatiel.
  Tamed with seeds like a budgie; the perch, the greeting, the shoulder and the call all work the
  same. Wild cockatiels live in savannas, in pairs and threes.
- The lovebird: a small, round little bird in three colours (peach-faced, Fischer's, the blue)
  that lives in a pair. Two lovebirds that meet pair up for good, keep together, call the same
  perch home and sit on it side by side. Their chirps are cut from recordings of real lovebirds.
  Wild lovebirds live in savannas, always two at a time.
- Chicks: feed seeds to two tamed birds of the same kind near each other and they make a chick,
  in one parent's colour, yours like its parents. It's small, follows its parents, grows up in
  about twenty minutes, and is too little for a shoulder until then. Lovebirds breed only with
  their partner.

## 0.1.0: Someone small is glad you're home

The first release, for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric and NeoForge.

- The budgie: a small pet bird in four colours (green, blue, yellow, white), tamed with seeds,
  that flies, sits when told and follows its owner. A spawn egg.
- The budgie's own model and animation: it flaps when it flies, steps and bobs its head on the
  ground, and settles down when told to sit. Textures with the real markings: the wild green's
  yellow face, barred back and violet cheek spots; the blue with a white face; the lutino yellow
  and the albino white with red eyes.
- Wild budgies in savannas and on jungle edges, in small flocks. A settings file
  (`config/little_birds.properties`) turns natural spawning off or makes it rarer.
- The perch, crafted from string, a stick and a plank. A tamed budgie makes the nearest perch its
  home: it keeps near it, sits on it now and then, sleeps on it every night with its head tucked
  in, and stays home when you go out instead of following. Break the perch and it follows you
  again.
- The greeting: when you come back after an hour or more away, your budgie chirps hello and
  bobs. On its perch after dark it goes quiet.
- The budgie's own voice: chatter, a hello chirp and a settling-down warble, several variants of
  each, cut from public-domain recordings of real budgies.
- The icon: a green budgie on its perch.
- Shoulder on request: sneak-use a tamed budgie with an empty hand and it sits on your shoulder;
  it never jumps off for a jump or a fall, and it comes with you through portals. Sneak-use it
  again, or sneak-use a block, to set it down. Use a perch to set a shoulder bird down on it, or to call your nearest budgie to it.
