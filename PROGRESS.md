# Progress

What's done, what's broken, what's next. Newest entry first. Each session adds a row before it
ends, saying what changed, what the game test counted, what the proof run was (the feature off,
which checks fail, code put back), what was learned, and what was deliberately not done.

## Where things stand

- **Done, birds:** every bird shares one base (the perch, the greeting, the shoulder, the call);
  each brings its size, colours, model and voice. The canary: a finch a touch smaller than the
  budgie in three colours, with a song (several seconds, cut from real canaries) every morning and
  for a millet spray, and no shoulder; wild in flower forests, meadows and cherry groves. The cockatiel: three colours, a crest that
  stands for the greeting, a whistle, savanna spawns. The lovebird: three colours, pairs that
  find each other, keep together and sit side by side on one perch, savanna spawns in twos.
  Chicks: two tamed birds of a kind fed seeds make one, in a parent's colour, half size, that
  grows up in about twenty minutes; lovebirds breed only with their partner.
- **Done:** the budgie: its own model and animation (flapping in the air, little steps and a head
  bob on the ground, settling down to sit, head tucked when asleep), textures with the real
  markings in four colours, a spawn egg with its own art; tamed with seeds, sits when told,
  follows its owner; wild budgies in savannas and jungles in small flocks, with a settings file to
  turn them off or make them rarer. The perch (string, stick, plank): a tamed budgie makes the
  nearest one its home, keeps near it, stays home when its owner goes out, sits on it now and
  then by day and sleeps on it every night, and is an ordinary pet again if the perch is broken.
  Game tests on both loaders cover all of it, alone and alongside Vibrant Parrots, Cozy Core and
  Cosy Critters; CI runs them on every push.
  The greeting: when its owner comes back after an hour or more away, the budgie chirps hello and
  bobs; asleep on its perch it is quiet. Shoulder on request: a sneak-use with an empty hand puts
  it on its owner's shoulder, where it stays through jumps and falls; a sneak-use on it or on a
  block sets it down; using a perch sets a shoulder bird down on it or calls the nearest budgie
  to it.
- **Done, sounds:** the budgie's voice is real: eleven short cuts (three hello chirps, five bits of
  chatter, three soft settling warbles) from CC0 recordings of pet budgies on Freesound, mono OGG
  in `sounds/budgie/`, wired in `sounds.json`. Gabby's own recordings can replace them file for file.
- **Done, icon:** a green budgie on its perch, `art/icon.png`, in the jars and the listing folder.
- **Placeholder:** none. The textures are drawn by `tools/draw-art.py`; hand-painted ones can
  replace them in the same layout.
- **Not yet:** nothing of 0.1.0's feature list; what is left is release prep.

## Next

1. The zebra finch (in twos and threes), the second song bird, then 0.3.0 (the eating features
   and the two song birds are unreleased on `main`). No cage: birds stay free and live on perches.
2. Gabby's listings on Modrinth and CurseForge (the kit in the listing folder is ready for
   0.2.0). Hand plays done 2026-11-04: the three birds, then the chicks.

New ideas during the build go to 0.2.0 or later, never into 0.1.0.
